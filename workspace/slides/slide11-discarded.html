Ideate: Discarded Ideas & Rationale

Digital Career Kiosks

Physical installations at community centres

Why Discarded:

  • High hardware and maintenance costs
  • Limited geographic reach
  • No scalability beyond installation sites
  • Dependent on physical infrastructure

WhatsApp Career Bot

Chatbot for basic career tips via WhatsApp

Why Discarded:

  • Limited functionality (text only)
  • Cannot generate documents
  • No structured interview practice
  • WhatsApp API costs are significant

Community Workshops

In-person facilitated career sessions

Why Discarded:

  • Not scalable nationally
  • High facilitator costs per user
  • Limited frequency and availability
  • No personalisation for individual needs
