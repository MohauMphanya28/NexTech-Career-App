Empathise: Research Methodology

One-on-One Interviews

Conducted 25+ interviews with unemployed youth across Kimberley and surrounding areas, aged 18–34

Local News & Labour Market Analysis

Reviewed Stats SA quarterly reports, local media coverage of youth unemployment trends and employer expectations

Social Media Listening

Monitored conversations on Facebook, X, and LinkedIn where young job seekers share frustrations and experiences

Community Engagement Sessions

Hosted 3 focus groups at community centres in Galeshewe, Homevale, and Roodepan to understand barriers first-hand
