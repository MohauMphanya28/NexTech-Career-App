Define: Root Cause Analysis — 5 Whys

1

Why are youth unemployed? They cannot successfully apply for and secure jobs.

2

Why can't they secure jobs? Their applications are rejected or never seen by employers.

3

Why are applications rejected? Resumes fail ATS filters and lack professional formatting.

4

Why do resumes fail? Youth lack knowledge of ATS systems, professional standards, and SA job market norms.

5

Root Cause: There is no accessible, affordable, AI-powered tool tailored to South African job seekers that guides them through the entire application process.
