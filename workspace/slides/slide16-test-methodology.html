Test: Testing Methodology

Participants

15 young job seekers (ages 19–31) from Kimberley, including matric graduates, diploma holders, and self-taught developers

Expert Reviewers

3 CAHAU representatives who sit on actual job interview boards and hiring panels in the Northern Cape

Testing Methods

  • Task-based usability testing
  • Think-aloud protocol
  • System Usability Scale (SUS)
  • Expert heuristic evaluation

Key Tasks Tested

  • Create a resume from scratch
  • Analyse and improve an existing CV
  • Generate a cover letter for a job
  • Complete a mock interview
